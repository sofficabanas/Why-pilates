Mat pilates

Pilates reformer

series of exercises done on the floor using your body weight to provide resistance, it's easier because it doesn't need any equipment, the aim is the same.

it consists of different exercises with equipment that focus on working out different parts of the body. The main equipment is the pilates bed, which has a sliding carriage, straps for hands and feet and adjustable springs made for people to choose personally on the work they want to perform.
